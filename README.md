A one-line comedy entry built around a quiet surrender. A grocery loyalty
card has logged a year of weekly cat-food purchases and the store's
algorithm has drawn the obvious conclusion; the narrator has not -- he
owns no cat. The joke turns in its final words, where the confession
lands: worn down by a year of targeted coupons, he is starting to come
around to the algorithm's version of his life. No pun, no telegraphed
payoff -- the ending is a change of heart you cannot see from the first
three words.

## The entry

> My loyalty card has seen me buy cat food every week for a year, so the store keeps sending me cat coupons; I don't own a cat, but honestly, I'm starting to consider one.

169 characters.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.
